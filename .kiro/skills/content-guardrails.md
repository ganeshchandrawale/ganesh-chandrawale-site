# Content Guardrails — Personal Capacity & Thought Leadership

This site is a personal portfolio and thought-leadership space. All content must comply with the following rules before publishing.

## Attribution & Positioning

- Do NOT mention employer names, client names, internal teams, or branded frameworks.
- Avoid language implying ownership of internal IP (e.g. "frameworks I developed", "what worked in our programme", "lessons learned from delivery").
- Use neutral phrasing: "architectural considerations", "patterns often seen", "one way to think about...".

## Experience Framing

- Do NOT claim "hands-on experience in enterprise programmes" or "production delivery".
- Frame experience as: practitioner perspective, architectural analysis, industry observation, or conceptual reasoning.
- Prefer "illustrative examples" over real-world anecdotes traceable to specific engagements.

## Models, Roadmaps, and Maturity Language

- Avoid numbered or formalised maturity models unless clearly labelled as illustrative.
- If using stages or levels, soften with: "one possible approach", "an illustrative lens", "a conceptual model".
- Avoid prescriptive or operational tone.
- Do not include diagrams or step-by-step implementation guidance.

## Industry & Regulation

- High-level industry references (e.g. regulated industries, insurance) are acceptable if generic and non-delivery-specific.
- Avoid production, compliance, or governance detail that could imply internal controls or specific programmes.

## Tone & Authority

- Sound like a senior architect explaining how to think, not how to implement.
- Focus on decision-making, trade-offs, and framing — not execution.
- Avoid absolute claims ("the correct way", "must", "the only approach").

## Author Bio

Use this neutral, reusable bio:

> "Solution architect focused on large-scale systems, API platforms, and emerging AI integration patterns."

Do not include employer names or client sectors in the bio.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

Before finalising any post, article, or page, verify:

- [ ] No explicit or implicit employer attribution
- [ ] No client or project traceability
- [ ] No internal IP or framework leakage
- [ ] Consistent tone with personal, independent thought leadership
- [ ] Author bio uses neutral form (no employer or client sector)

If any section risks inference, rewrite it to be more abstract, illustrative, or analytical.

## Footer Disclaimer

Every page must include this footer note verbatim:

> Important note: This website is a personal portfolio and thought-leadership space.
> All opinions, interpretations, and perspectives expressed here are my own and are shared in a personal capacity. Nothing on this site should be interpreted as representing the views, practices, or intellectual property of my employer or any past or present client.
